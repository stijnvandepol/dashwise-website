Dashwise privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Dashwise is made by Stijn van de Pol, the Netherlands. This policy explains what happens with data in the Dashwise app for iPhone. This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics.

The short version

Dashwise does not collect, store or share any personal data on a server. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything Dashwise records stays on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Engine data read from your OBD-II adapter (for example engine speed, speed, temperatures, fault codes).
  • Trips you record: engine data over time and, if you allow location, the route, lap times and the start and end address.
  • Your settings, such as your adapter, units, warning limits and tracks.
  • Your car's VIN, if the car reports it, to look up which manufacturer codes apply. It is stored only on your iPhone and is never written to logs.

Trip files are stored in the Dashwise folder of the Files app and are excluded from iCloud and iPhone backups. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth: to connect to your OBD-II adapter. While a trip records, and briefly after you leave the app, the connection stays active in the background.
  • Location (While Using the App): only if you allow it, for the speed, the route of a trip and lap times. Dashwise asks only when you choose a location feature, and keeps recording a trip you started when you switch apps (iOS shows the location indicator then).
  • Local network (Wi-Fi adapters): to talk to the adapter on its own Wi-Fi network.

Addresses

The trip logbook shows a start and an end address for each trip. To find them, your iPhone sends only the start point and the end point of the trip to Apple, in the same way as the Maps app, and Apple returns the address. The route, the time of the trip, your engine data and any name are not sent; Apple handles the lookup under its own privacy policy. The address is stored on your iPhone only, next to the trip, and you can edit it. Without an internet connection or without a location fix, Dashwise tries again later or shows coordinates, or leaves the address empty.

Maps

The trip detail can show the route on a map from Apple Maps (MapKit). While that screen is open, your iPhone downloads map images for the area shown, the same way any app with an Apple map does. The route itself, your trips and your engine data are never sent. You can hide the map; Dashwise then draws the route by itself, without loading any map.

Sharing

Dashwise never sends data anywhere by itself. You can export a trip (CSV, GPX, VBO), a mileage log (CSV, with the start and end addresses) or the debug log through the iOS share sheet; before you share, Dashwise shows what the file contains. What happens after you share is up to you and the app or person you share with.

Children

Dashwise is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
