OBD-II dashboard for iPhone

OBD2 app for iPhone. Your car, at a glance.

Dashwise is an OBD2 app for iPhone that shows live engine data from a Bluetooth or Wi-Fi OBD adapter. Read fault codes, keep a trip logbook and time laps, all private on your iPhone.

Live dashboard

An OBD-II dashboard you can read at a glance.

Large numerals made to be read at a glance, and a smooth segmented rev bar with redline and caution zones for your engine.

  • Engine speed, road speed, coolant, intake, throttle, load, battery voltage and more
  • A sensors list with every value your car supports
  • Your own limits for coolant, voltage and rpm: a calm coloured frame, a banner and an optional tone when a value goes out of range

Fault codes

Read fault codes and know what the check-engine light means.

Read stored, pending and permanent diagnostic trouble codes with plain-English descriptions.

  • Readiness monitors and freeze frame data
  • Clear codes safely: only with the engine off and after two confirmations

Clearing codes also resets the readiness monitors. Fix the cause first.

Trip logbook

A trip logbook and mileage log for every drive.

Record trips automatically or by hand. Distance, duration, top speed and the route of every drive, with start and end addresses.

  • Export your trips for mileage claims
  • Export to GPX, VBO or CSV and share with the apps you already use
  • Addresses are looked up on your iPhone with Apple and stay on your iPhone

Track days

Lap timing for the circuit.

GPS lap timing with your own start/finish and sector lines. Best lap, last lap and live delta, plus a shift light you set yourself.

Lap timing is meant for circuits, not public roads.

Dashwise shows what your car reports. Keep your eyes on the road and let a passenger operate the app while driving.

Private by design

Your data stays on your iPhone.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your trips and engine data are never uploaded.

  • Engine data and trips are stored on your device only
  • Map images come from Apple Maps while a trip's map is on screen; the route itself is never sent
  • Only the start and end points of a trip are sent to Apple to look up their addresses

What you need

An OBD adapter and a car.

An adapter

A Bluetooth LE or Wi-Fi OBD-II adapter that works with iPhone. See the support page for details.

A car

A car with an OBD-II port: most petrol cars from 1996 in the US and 2001 in the EU, diesels from 2004.

Not Classic Bluetooth

Classic Bluetooth adapters without "Made for iPhone" support cannot connect to an iPhone. This is an iOS limit, not a Dashwise one.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Does Dashwise work with my car?

Most likely, if it has an OBD-II port: that covers most petrol cars from 1996 in the US and 2001 in the EU, and diesels from 2004. Which values show depends on what your car reports. Dashwise was tested on a Toyota Corolla (ISO 9141-2, K-line) with a Bluetooth LE OBD adapter.

Which OBD2 adapters work on iPhone?

A Bluetooth LE or Wi-Fi OBD adapter. Classic Bluetooth adapters without Apple's "Made for iPhone" chip cannot connect to an iPhone; that is an iOS limit. See which adapters work.

Can I read and clear fault codes with Dashwise?

Yes. Dashwise reads stored, pending and permanent diagnostic trouble codes with plain-English descriptions, plus readiness monitors and freeze frame data. Clearing codes only works with the engine off and after two confirmations.

Can I use Dashwise as a trip logbook?

Yes. It records trips automatically or by hand, with distance, duration, top speed, the route and start and end addresses, and exports them to GPX, VBO or CSV, plus a mileage log you can send to your employer.

Does Dashwise collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Engine data and trips stay on your iPhone. Only the start and end points of a trip are sent to Apple to look up their addresses. See the privacy policy.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. An Android version will follow and will look and work like the iPhone app.
